## 1. Brand Identity

Redvia, formally presented in source materials as **RedVia-BRY International Co.**, is the goji-focused international sub-brand and global expansion platform of **Bairuiyuan / 百瑞源 (BRY)**.

Redvia's role is to bring Bairuiyuan's goji products, goji-processing capability, research background, and brand values to international markets. The 2026 product catalog describes RedVia-BRY International Co. as the dedicated international expansion arm and exclusive global gateway for Bairuiyuan, responsible for cross-border distribution, overseas market development, and global partnership building.

## 2. Parent Brand: 百瑞源 / Bairuiyuan

Bairuiyuan is a comprehensive goji berry enterprise founded in 2003. Its business covers goji berry technology research and development, organic cultivation, production and processing, marketing, and goji culture tourism.

Source materials list the following recognitions and assets:

- China Famous Trademark.
- National Key Leading Enterprise in Agricultural Industrialization.
- National High-tech Enterprise.
- National AAAA-rated Tourist Attraction.

- More than 100 offline brand image stores.

## 3. Brand Positioning

Redvia positions goji as a premium botanical ingredient for functional wellness, daily nutrition, beverage innovation, beauty nutrition, and ingredient applications. The product catalog emphasizes origin, active compounds, product safety, processing capability, and global-market readiness.

### Positioning Themes

- **Origin and authenticity:** Ningxia red goji is positioned as an origin-specific premium goji ingredient.
- **Functional nutrition:** Product materials emphasize polysaccharides, zeaxanthin, carotenoids, polyphenols, flavonoids, anthocyanins, amino acids, and other plant compounds.
- **International ingredient supply:** Redvia is framed as the route for Bairuiyuan products to reach global partners.
- **Scientific credibility:** Parent-brand materials describe long-term cooperation with universities and research institutes on goji polysaccharides, carotenoids, eye health, liver protection, and goji processing technologies.
- **Quality and compliance:** Product sheets reference BRC, HACCP, ISO9001, ISO14001, CNAS laboratory accreditation, organic certifications, and export-related certificates.

## 5. Product Details From Specifications

### Dried Red Goji / Dried Wolfberry

- Origin: China.
- Processing: hot-air drying of fresh, healthy, mature cultivated wolfberry fruits; grading, color sorting, hair/fiber removal, metal detection, UV sterilization, X-ray inspection, manual sorting, packaging, boxing, and inspection.
- Key indicators: moisture <= 13.0%; sulfur dioxide <= 30.0 mg/kg; lead <= 1.0 mg/kg; cadmium <= 0.3 mg/kg.
- Nutrition per 100 g: 387 kcal, protein 12.5 g, fat 3.8 g, carbohydrates 75.6 g, total sugars 48.6 g, wolfberry polysaccharides 3.48 g, betaine 0.867 g.
- GMO status: Non-GMO.

### Red Goji Puree

- Use: raw material for beverage and food processing.
- Processing: crushing, pulping, homogenization, formulation, sterilization, and filling from fresh cultivated wolfberry fruits.
- Key indicators: soluble solids >= 15.0%; total acidity >= 0.4 g/100 g; wolfberry polysaccharides >= 0.5 g/100 g; betaine >= 0.15 g/100 g.
- Nutrition per 100 g: 322 kcal, protein 2.64 g, fat 0.3 g, carbohydrates 16.3 g, total sugars 15.6 g.
- Storage: below -10 C.
- GMO status: Non-GMO.

### Black Goji Puree

- Use: raw material for beverage and food processing.
- Processing: made from dried black wolfberry fruits through washing, extraction, formulation, sterilization, and filling.
- Key indicators: soluble solids >= 8.0%; total acidity >= 0.3 g/100 g; proanthocyanidins >= 0.1%.
- Nutrition per 100 g: 136 kcal, protein 0.9 g, fat 0 g, carbohydrates 7.1 g.
- Storage: cool and dry place.
- GMO status: Non-GMO.

### Black Goji Berry

- Use: direct brewing and consumption.
- Processing: fresh mature black wolfberry fruits are sun-dried, then graded, cleaned of hair/fibers, metal detected, X-ray inspected, sorted, packaged, and inspected.
- Key indicators: moisture <= 12 g/100 g; proanthocyanidins >= 1.0 g/100 g; sulfur dioxide <= 30 mg/kg; lead <= 0.5 mg/kg; pathogenic bacteria absent.
- Nutrition per 100 g: 1607 kJ, protein 11.8 g, fat 5.9 g, carbohydrates 69.9 g, sugar 38.4 g.
- GMO status: Non-GMO.

### Goji Polysaccharide Powder

- Use: raw material for food processing.
- Processing: water-soluble polysaccharide prepared from dried wolfberry fruits through water extraction, solid-liquid separation, concentration, ethanol precipitation, drying, packaging, boxing, and inspection.
- Key indicators: wolfberry polysaccharides >= 50.0 g/100 g; moisture <= 5.0 g/100 g; lead <= 0.5 mg/kg; aerobic plate count <= 1000 CFU/g; coliforms <= 0.92 MPN/g; molds <= 50 CFU/g; Salmonella and Staphylococcus aureus absent in 25 g.
- GMO status: Non-GMO.

### Goji Leaf Tea

- Use: brewed directly or used as raw material for food processing.
- Processing: fresh goji leaves and buds through withering, fixation, cooling, roasting, color sorting, sorting, packaging, X-ray inspection, and testing.
- Key indicators: moisture <= 8.0 g/100 g; lead <= 5.0 mg/kg.
- Nutrition per 100 g: protein 46.7 g, fat 6.0 g, carbohydrates 33.0 g, sodium 892 mg.
- GMO status: Non-GMO.

### Goji Leaf Matcha Powder / Goji Tea Powder

- Main ingredient: goji leaves.
- Processing: fresh goji leaves processed by high-temperature fixation and special techniques; no food additives or processing aids are added.
- Key indicators: green or yellowish-green powder, no caking, characteristic goji leaf aroma, no off-odor; fineness options of 80 mesh, 200 mesh, and 500 mesh; moisture <= 8.0%; lead <= 0.5 mg/kg; total arsenic <= 0.5 mg/kg.

### Goji Seed Oil

- Raw material: wolfberry seeds.
- Processing: supercritical CO2 extraction.
- Key indicators: orange or orange-red color, clear and transparent at 20 C, moisture and volatiles <= 0.20%, insoluble impurities <= 0.20%, acid value <= 10.0 mg KOH/g.
- Fatty acid composition: palmitic acid 5.5-7.0%, stearic acid 2.5-4.0%, oleic acid 16.0-23.0%, linoleic acid 63.0-70.0%, gamma-linolenic acid 1.5-3.0%, alpha-linolenic acid 0.5-1.5%.

## 12. Suggested Brand Messaging

### One-Sentence Brand Description

Redvia is the international goji brand of Bairuiyuan, connecting premium Chinese goji ingredients, certified production, and research-backed functional nutrition concepts with global food, beverage, supplement, and beauty-wellness partners.

### Short Boilerplate

Redvia is the international sub-brand of Bairuiyuan (百瑞源), a comprehensive goji enterprise founded in 2003. Backed by Bairuiyuan's cultivation bases, certified processing facilities, R&D platforms, and goji culture assets, Redvia supplies goji berries, goji purees, goji leaf tea, goji polysaccharide powder, goji seed oil, and other goji-based ingredients for global wellness, beverage, functional food, and beauty-nutrition applications.

### Claim-Safe Messaging Principles

- Use "supports", "positioned for", "suitable for", and "used in formulations for" instead of disease-treatment language.
- Separate ingredient composition from health outcomes.
- Treat clinical, animal, and in vitro research as scientific background, not direct consumer claims.
- Review every export-market claim against local regulations before packaging, advertising, or distributor use.
